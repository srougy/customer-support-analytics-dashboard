## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an **interactive Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze and monitor **call center operations and customer service performance**. The dashboard transforms raw operational data into meaningful insights, enabling stakeholders to evaluate efficiency, track KPIs, and improve decision-making.

The solution focuses on delivering a **data-driven view of customer support performance**, highlighting operational strengths, inefficiencies, and opportunities for optimization.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Monitor **call center performance** in real-time
* Evaluate **service efficiency and responsiveness**
* Track **key performance indicators (KPIs)**
* Analyze **agent productivity and workload distribution**
* Identify areas for **operational improvement and cost reduction**

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes critical call center metrics such as:

* 📞 **Total Calls** — overall call volume
* ⏱️ **Average Speed of Answer (ASA)** — responsiveness to incoming calls
* 🎯 **Service Level Agreement (SLA)** — percentage of calls answered within target time
* ❌ **Abandonment Rate** — missed or dropped calls
* ⏳ **Average Handling Time (AHT)** — efficiency of call resolution
* 👨‍💼 **Agent Performance Metrics** — productivity and efficiency per agent

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

* Interactive filters (date, project, agent, etc.)
* Dynamic KPI tracking and comparisons
* Time-series analysis of call trends
* Performance breakdown by agent and project
* Visual identification of best and worst-performing segments

---

## 🧠 Key Insights

* Identification of **peak call periods** and workload distribution
* Detection of **performance gaps across agents or projects**
* Insight into **service delays and bottlenecks**
* Evaluation of **customer service efficiency over time**
